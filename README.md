# Datheon Data Analytics & Power BI Internship

## Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – Superstore Retail Sales

# Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the Datheon Data Analytics & Power BI Internship.

The objective is to explore Superstore retail sales data, identify business trends, analyze customer purchasing behaviour, and understand factors affecting profitability.

# Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

# Project Files
- `Datheon_Task1_EDA.ipynb` – Complete Python code, analysis, visualizations, and findings.
- `cleaned_superstore.csv` – Cleaned Superstore retail sales dataset.

# Work Performed
1. Data cleaning and preprocessing.
2. Customer purchase volume analysis.
3. Monthly sales trend analysis.
4. Category-wise revenue analysis.
5. Regional sales and profitability analysis.
6. Discount impact on profitability.
7. Business insights and recommendations.

# Key Findings
- Technology generated the highest category-wise revenue.
- The West region recorded the highest sales and profit.
- Emily Phan placed the highest number of unique orders.
- Higher discount levels were generally associated with lower average profits.
- The South region had the lowest total sales.

# Overall Business Performance
- Total Sales: 2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: 286,397.02
- Total Quantity Sold: 37,873
- Total Orders: 5,009

# Conclusion
The analysis identified important patterns in sales, customer behaviour, regional performance, and discount impact. These insights can help businesses improve sales strategies and profitability.
